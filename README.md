## My Background in Cybersecurity

I've always been fascinated by understanding how software works, particularly how seemingly secure systems can contain weaknesses that attackers might exploit.

Cybersecurity is an essential and constantly evolving discipline. As malicious actors discover increasingly sophisticated methods of compromising systems, security researchers must continually develop new approaches to identifying and mitigating vulnerabilities.

My philosophy is simple:

**To defend a system effectively, you must first understand how it can be compromised.**

This philosophy has motivated much of my academic work and independent research.

## From Cybersecurity to Artificial Intelligence

During my doctoral studies in computer science, I became particularly interested in exploring whether **mathematics, computational methods, algorithmic patterns, and system-state analysis** could be combined to discover previously unknown software vulnerabilities.

My proposed research focused on identifying latent zero-day vulnerabilities that conventional security tools and manual analysis might overlook.

This interest eventually led me into artificial intelligence and machine learning.

I began exploring how AI algorithms could potentially automate or enhance vulnerability discovery by recognizing complex patterns, analyzing system behavior, and identifying anomalous conditions.

I subsequently placed my doctoral studies on indefinite hold to pursue graduate studies in artificial intelligence, with the long-term goal of integrating both disciplines into a unified research approach.
